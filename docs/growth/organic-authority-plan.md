# Organic authority plan: free, genuine links and mentions

No paid links, sponsored placements, link schemes, fake reviews or mass outreach. Every item below is free, relevant, and something a real business would do anyway. As of 27 Nov 2026.

**What "verified free" means here.** I read the site's own page on 27 Nov 2026 and it says a basic listing is free. Everything else is **to verify** at sign-up. If it asks for payment, skip it.

**Use the same description everywhere** (from `organic-search-master-plan.md` §5), with the same logo and the link `https://garnishtable.com`.

## 2. Links that come from real customers (the strongest source)
- **"Online ordering by GarnishTable" link:** checked 27 Nov 2026: storefront footers say "Powered by a platform built for real restaurants" but don't name GarnishTable or link to `garnishtable.com`. A small product change would let every storefront earn one relevant link: name it and link it in the footer, except where white-label branding is hidden (the themes already have a `hideBranding` switch). **Not built yet; needs your go-ahead.**
- **The restaurant's own website and Google Business Profile:** when they add their ordering link, it points to their storefront, which links back.
- **Case studies:** once a launch restaurant has real results, write them up on garnishtable.com (with their written permission). The restaurant often shares or links it.

## 3. Partnerships (free, by relationship)
- **Local web designers and marketing agencies** that build restaurant websites: they can resell or recommend GarnishTable. The Agency — Growth plan exists for exactly this. A partner page listing them, plus their link back, is a fair exchange.
- **Restaurant consultants and accountants** who advise independents on margins: offer them the commission calculator and a short guide to share.
- **Local business associations or chambers of commerce** in the launch city: a member directory listing, if membership is free.

## 4. Content that earns links on its own
- **Commission calculator:** a free tool people link to when discussing delivery-app fees.
- **Ordering software checklist:** a one-page downloadable PDF (commission, fees, contracts, hardware, payment processing).
- **Original data, later:** once there are enough restaurants, publish an anonymised stat (e.g. the share of orders from direct links versus QR codes). Only with real data.

## 5. Communities (helping, not advertising)
- Restaurant-owner subreddits and forums, local restaurant Facebook groups, and LinkedIn: answer questions about direct ordering, commissions and setup in full.
- Mention GarnishTable only where it's directly relevant, and say you built it. Read each community's self-promotion rules first and follow them.
- Budget: 2–3 genuinely useful answers a week. Track replies and visits in `seo-performance-tracker.md`.

## 6. Weekly rhythm (about 2 hours)
- One new listing or profile (until §1 is done).
- One partnership conversation (§3).
- 2–3 community answers (§5).
- Log everything in the tracker's "Authority" tab.
