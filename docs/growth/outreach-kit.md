# First-five outreach kit (US and UK, remote)

Ready to use once you approve each contact. **Nothing here is sent, posted or created automatically.** Every message is written for one restaurant and sent by you. As of 27 Nov 2026. It goes with `us-uk-market-plan.md` §5 and `first-five-campaign.md`.

## 1. Choosing the two cities (a 2-hour test, not a guess)

I haven't measured restaurant counts or competitor share for any city. Pick by **testing**, not fame.

**Candidates (UK):** Manchester, Birmingham, Leeds. **Candidates (US, East Coast):** Philadelphia, Boston, Baltimore.
East Coast because a 9–11am ET demo is 7–9pm in Pakistan; West Coast hours are harder to cover.

**Test each candidate (20 minutes per city):** on Google Maps, search "takeaway" (UK) or "takeout" (US) and "café" in two central neighbourhoods. Open the first 30 independents (skip chains). Count:

| Signal | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| A = no ordering link at all, or "call to order" | Strong fit: they need an ordering page, and pay-at-pickup is already their habit |
| B = only a Just Eat / Deliveroo / Uber Eats / DoorDash link | Good fit if collection or pickup is a big part of their trade |
| C = already has direct ordering (Flipdish, Slerp, ChowNow, Toast, Square) | Poor fit for now |
| D = has a website and/or active Instagram | We can reach them and show a preview |

**Pick the city in each country with the highest A+B among D.** Write the counts in the tracker. That count is the market evidence.

**Segments that fit today's product** (customers pay at pickup, delivery or the table):
- collection- and phone-order-heavy **takeaways** (pizza, kebab, chippy, Chinese, Indian);
- **cafés and bakeries** with pre-order;
- **counter-service** places;
- **pubs with food** (UK) that want table ordering by QR code.

**Avoid for now:** delivery-led restaurants whose online orders are mostly prepaid. Without online card payments they'd hit our biggest gap.

## 2. Preparing a preview (about 20 minutes, needs your platform admin)
1. **Once:** a reusable **preview restaurant** owned by an alias you control (`first-five-campaign.md` §4.1).
2. **For each prospect:** in that preview restaurant, **import their public menu** (website URL, PDF or photos), check prices, set their colour, publish.
3. **Record a 60–90 second screen video:** their menu on a phone, adding an item, the order arriving on the kitchen screen. Free tools: Loom's free tier, or a phone screen recording.
4. Paste the video link into the tracker row.

## 3. Lawful channel per prospect
| Market / business type | First contact |
|---|---|
| US, any business | Email to the **published business address**. Include your name, a postal address and a one-line opt-out (CAN-SPAM). |
| UK, **limited company** (check Companies House; "Ltd" in their name or on their site) | Email to the published business address, with an opt-out line |
| UK, **sole trader or partnership** (most takeaways) | **No cold email.** Use the website contact form, an Instagram DM (within Instagram's rules) or a phone call to the business number. Email only after they've said yes to hearing more. |

One message per prospect, one follow-up after 3 days, then stop.

## 5. The 15-minute demo
1. **2 minutes:** how they take orders today; what annoys them (fees, missed calls).
2. **5 minutes:** their menu on the preview, an order placed by phone, the order arriving on the kitchen screen, Accept → Complete.
3. **3 minutes:** setup. Ordering link on the Google profile and Instagram bio, a QR code at the counter, hours and collection or delivery settings.
4. **2 minutes:** price, the 14-day trial with no card, cancel anytime. Customers pay them directly; online card payment is coming.
5. **3 minutes:** if yes, book the 30-minute onboarding (`first-five-campaign.md` §4.3) within 3 days.

## 6. Tracker
Use `outreach-tracker-template.csv` (same folder) in Google Sheets. **One row per restaurant, never deleted.** Funnel stages:
`researched → preview ready → contacted → replied → demo booked → demo done → trial started → first real order → paid` (or `lost: reason`).

**Weekly numbers:** contacted, reply rate, demos, trials, **trials with a real order in their first 7 days**, paid, MRR.
